Security — Access Control (ADMIN ONLY) Access is determined by the account role (D1 auth_users), not by an allowlist
Access vocabulary: ADMIN means the account can manage users and settings; REGISTERED means the account can view and update operational data; READ ONLY means the account can view data but cannot save changes. Your level comes from your account role, not from an email list.
