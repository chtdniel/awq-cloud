AWQ Cloud Briefing

Flight briefing workspace for dispatch operations: monitor flight watch, manage NOTAM and FIR, review TAF and weather, then produce a crew briefing report — downloaded as XLSX or created directly as a Google Sheet in your own Drive account.

Open workspace View capabilities Access requires a registered account.

What you can do

One place to assemble the operational flight picture before briefing and before dispatch.

Flight watch

Daily flight board with per-flight status, DOF, STD/STA, and flagging of the flights that need attention.

NOTAM & FIR

NOTAM updates, change history, FIR map, plus NOTAM analysis you can select and save for the briefing.

TAF & weather

Per-airport TAF, departure/arrival time windows, and weather warnings relevant to the route.

Crew briefing report

One click to build the report: download XLSX, or create a Google Sheet in your own Google account Drive. Every click produces a new report.

Route & waypoint

Manage routes and registered waypoint markers to close route-data gaps before a report is generated.

Access control

Account roles (admin, registered, read only) determine who may view and who may change operational data.

Account and access

This application is used by operations personnel who are already registered. There is no self sign-up on this page.

  1. Request an account. Contact the operations administrator so an account and its role can be created for you.
  2. Sign in to the workspace. Open /app and sign in with the email and password you were given.
  3. Generate a report. Select the flights and NOTAMs, then produce the report as XLSX or Google Sheets.

About Google Sheets. When you choose to create the report as a Google Sheet, the application asks for a single Google permission — drive.file — which grants access only to files this application creates or opens, not the entire contents of your Drive. The access token is used in your browser for that upload and is never stored on the application server.

Details are in the Privacy Policy. You can revoke the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
